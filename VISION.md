# Vision

## Purpose

> **Explorer** *(noun)*: a seeker of horizons who steps beyond the edge of the known world, charting untamed lands, plunging into silent oceans and reaching toward the stars, while equally navigating the vast, unmapped frontiers of human thought, science and imagination to bring hidden truths into the light.
>
> This is my purpose in life.

In data, exploring means the same thing: taking raw, unmapped signals (sensor readings, fault messages, maintenance logs) and turning them into something people can see and act on. Aerospace is where I want to do that, because the stakes are real: an aircraft on the ground, an engine that degrades faster than expected, a decision a maintenance team has to take tonight.

## Principles I work by

1. **The business need comes first.** Before coding, I restate the problem in the user's words: who acts on this, how fast, and what a wrong answer costs.
2. **One finished slice beats five started modules.** Aerolisa ships one use case end to end before the next one starts.
3. **Fundamentals before tools.** SQL, cardinality, layers and quality rules transfer to any platform; a tool badge does not.
4. **Fail loudly, never silently.** A pipeline stops rather than publishing data it cannot trust. In maintenance, a wrong number is a safety issue, not a cosmetic one.
5. **Version everything.** I once lost a week of work to an automatic update. Since then, everything is saved to the cloud and versioned daily.
6. **Say only what is true today.** Status in this repository is updated as work progresses, never in advance.
7. **Consistency over intensity.** Fixed weekly blocks (mathematics on Saturdays, computer science on Sundays) and English every day, including on a 4-hour daily commute.
