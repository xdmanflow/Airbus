## Where Skywise apprentices actually work

"Skywise" means two things, and most apprenticeship offers are in the second:

1. **Skywise the company** (since April 2026): digital products sold to airlines.
2. **Skywise as Airbus's internal big data platform**, used across engineering, quality, procurement, customer services and manufacturing, in Airbus Commercial, Airbus Operations SAS, Airbus Atlantic and other entities.

**Typical offers seen in 2025–2026:** data applications developer (PySpark or SQL in Foundry on SAP data, apps in Slate and Workshop), data scientist for customer-services KPIs on Skywise, data analyst building Skywise dashboards for engineering teams, plus data and AI roles in Defence and Space, operations and quality.

**What they share:** Python and SQL, PySpark in Foundry, dashboards and apps, and explaining data to non-specialists in English.
