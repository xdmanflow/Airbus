## Communication

What separates junior profiles is rarely the code: it is understanding the business and explaining clearly.

- **Start with the answer.** For managers: "You hired X% of plan; here is where the gap is." Then only what they need to trust the number.
- **Explain without jargon.** A data pipeline, to a pilot: like fuel, collected from many places, checked, filtered and delivered on time for every flight; and like a pre-flight check, it stops and warns when something looks wrong.
- **Say "I'll check" rather than guess.** With data, a wrong answer given confidently is worse than a short delay.
- **Practise out loud:** I present Aerolisa regularly, in French and in English, under two minutes per story.
